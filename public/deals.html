How we decide what counts

01 · Match the product

Match the listed set, collector number, name, language, grade and stated condition. Compare only the same sealed product format and quantity.

02 · Compare total asking prices

Use the listing price plus known postage in the same currency. Unknown shipping is excluded, and import taxes are not included.

03 · Require multiple sellers

Require at least three matching active Buy It Now offers from different sellers. A conservative lower-quartile price guards against inflated outliers.

04 · Inspect evidence

Open the comparison listings and check condition, photos, authenticity, seller feedback and postage. Final bidding or buying happens on eBay.

Data source: the existing authorised eBay Browse API. Comparisons use active Buy It Now asking prices, not sold transactions. No TCGplayer, PriceCharting or Collectr prices are claimed or scraped. This is an opportunity finder, not a professional valuation service.
