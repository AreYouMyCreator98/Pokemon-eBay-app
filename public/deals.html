How we decide what counts

01 · Match the exact product

Same card number, set, printing, language, condition and grader/grade. Sealed products require an equivalent factory-sealed quantity.

02 · Compare the real cost

Item price plus known shipping, in the same currency. Unknown postage, taxes and currency conversions are never silently guessed.

03 · Check reference quality

Market references must be licensed, less than seven days old and attributed. Related data sources aren't counted as independent evidence.

04 · Review on eBay

Always check seller history, photos, authenticity, returns, postage and final availability. Purchasing and bidding stay on eBay.
